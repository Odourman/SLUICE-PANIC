Tester, date, console model/revision, TV format, cartridge/flashcart or RetroN77 firmware and Stella version, joystick model, display and audio, ROM SHA256, session duration. Verify hashes before loading. Test both regions where hardware permits; don't claim unsupported format as PASS. Cold boot three times; title/record; Classic start; move up/down, hold/release FIRE, port changes, cargo/debris outcomes; Game Over, hold SELECT across death, release/repress, Expert start; reset and session record; progress to four lanes; bonus and life behavior as encountered; audio clarity, image stability, flicker, HUD, HMOVE; 30 min sustained play per supported format; report subjective difficulty separately. Capture photos/video and controller sequence for failure. Log per item PASS/FAIL/NOT_RUN with evidence, console and exact hash. Test original 2600 and RetroN77 separately. Human players record learning time, confusion and reactions; no synthetic substitute. Do not infer commercial release from a pass.
